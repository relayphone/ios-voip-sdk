# R2: concrete ABI guards — candidate handoff

**Proposed, ready for final acceptance:** install verifies all 22 CallKit hooks on the resolved concrete provider, controller, observer and supported action classes. Provider and controller initialization uses separate public and concrete original implementations, correct retained/consumed ARC conventions and outermost-only registration. The action hierarchy captures original implementations before mutation and hooks every covered superclass so original-to-super forwarding runs once.

The snapshot preflight toggles and reads back all four KVC keys and catches Objective-C exceptions. Unsupported selectors and snapshot failures retain inactive report/request guards: a diagnostic `CKSimRuntimeErrorDomain` error, empty snapshots and inert mutators. They never restore genuine report/request methods after refusal. Shadow mode refuses differing concrete incoming/delegate originals before binding. PushKit's three hooks also preflight and verify their concrete implementations.

**Measured:** [R2 evidence](../verification/2026-10-22-runtime-r2/README.md) includes the v0.1 concrete incoming-report failure and eight passing primary scenarios with 13 checks each. That passing capture predates the final action-superclass forwarding fix and does not qualify it. The final candidate compiles for arm64 and x86_64; both fault controls are absent from shipping builds. Standards and Spec reviews are clean after addressing their forwarding and missing-selector-test findings.

**Pending acceptance:** the frozen eight-case, 18-check local run and CI, including the existing normal shadow push lane. The extended probe compares untracked fulfillment, failure, deadline and dated fulfillment with genuine preload references, and checks original dispatch through leaf/intermediate/inheriting-sibling action classes. Its final native receipts will follow before merge.

No RFC 0001 API deviation, upstream action, license addition or R3 teardown change is included. Device and LCK measurements remain separate acceptance targets.
