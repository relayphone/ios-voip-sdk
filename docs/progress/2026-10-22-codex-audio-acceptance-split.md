# Audio acceptance split for A0.3

**Proposed acceptance change, shipping SDK unchanged:** separate the iOS 27 LCK audio-authority journey from ordinary-session notification transport. The previous strict simulator diagnostic remains RED: its owned runs observed no modern notifications after a real LCK join. Its `validate_lifecycle` function and original negative controls are retained. The [physical ordinary-session packet](../research/callkit-simulator-probe/audio-session-lifecycle/2026-10-22-iphone13-ios27/README.md) also retains its full four-operation fixture RED; the third operation's engine stopped and the fourth operation never ran.

Apple documents [session lifecycle notifications](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/avfaudio/handling-audio-interruptions) and [LCK activation callbacks](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/livecommunicationkit/conversationmanagerdelegate/conversationmanager(_:didactivate:)) separately. Treating their documentation as a guarantee of one notification per LCK answer/hold/resume/end within five seconds was an inference, not a measured producer contract. No SDK activation policy changes follow from this split.

- **Proposed modern native acceptance:** `native-lck-audio-authority-v1` requires incoming → genuine LCK join → observed pending-empty, untimestamped `connecting` → explicit backend connected report → timestamped `connected` → hold → resume → ordinary End → empty state. Four real runtime actions, two real activation/deactivation cycles, handler/delegate/adapter authorization order, final revocation and normal cleanup remain mandatory. The fixture no longer calls `setActive` after the call.
- **Passive observations:** actual modern notification rows, when present, must carry matching C/Swift scalars, main delivery and synchronous inactive revocation. Active/recommendation rows cannot grant authorization. Zero rows report `notificationTransportExercised: false`; they provide no notification-delivery evidence. No Apple contexts or modern notifications are manufactured.
- **Measured transport slice:** the committed iPhone 13 / iOS 27.0 packet contains matching raw Apple/C/actual Swift-consumer inventories (2 active, 3 inactive), including real typed app deactivation contexts. The new offline validator binds its manifest, fourteen copied source/project files, eight unchanged current production audio files, signed executable/run/PID/trace and recorded local cleanup custody. This validates scalar transport only; the full physical probe stays failed, recommendation and hardware LCK interruption stay unmeasured, and private receipt hashes are not CI attestation.
- **Legacy acceptance:** the existing native synthetic interruption/recommendation wiring remains unchanged. The strict modern diagnostic and legacy gate remain separate from the new authority verdict. The four M1 contracts, six PushKit entry cases and separate LiveKit/watchdog/reentrancy/M2 case inventories are preserved.

**Measured offline verification:** the [source-bound receipt](../verification/2026-10-22-audio-acceptance-split/offline.json) records packet tamper controls, strict/authority gate controls and the integrated M2 synthetic/runner controls. These execute no compiler, app, simulator or device. M2 CI now requires the existing actual C-decoder host/SDK-declaration harness and the physical-packet validator, with packet changes included in its triggers and shared source identity.

**Still pending:** a fresh full modern LCK-authority capture, the legacy native wiring capture, current integrated native M2 and pod-built Release `iphoneos` acceptance. Genuine system-source interruption/nested reason, resumption recommendation, hardware LCK interruption/recovery, provider media, stall timing and a completed standalone async four-operation probe require separate measurements. The hardware engine-stop cause remains unknown. This change does not claim A0.3 or M2 completion; all earlier REDs and source-limited GREENs retain their original scope.
