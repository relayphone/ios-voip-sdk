# Runtime R7: device-derived acceptance expectations

Status: prepared for integration after R1, R2, R5 and R6. This change does not
claim a new simulator or device run.

## Changes

- Measured reference: the eight committed
  `docs/research/callkit-simulator-probe/provider-routing/2026-10-21-iphone13-ios27-providers-*.jsonl`
  captures supply action deadline samples. The comparator pools them by action
  kind and rejects missing, invalid or ambiguous references. In `single`, answer
  was delivered with 58.9 seconds remaining; other captures measured 60 seconds.
- Proposed qualification policy, implemented by the check: each band's upper
  bound is the largest measured sample plus 0.1 seconds for probe rounding; its
  lower bound is that sample minus 2 seconds for delegate scheduling. The
  tolerance is not a measurement of Apple's scheduling jitter.
- Measured D5 reference: the successful
  `device-system-ui/2026-10-20-iphone13-ui10-app.jsonl` phases supply Hold & Accept,
  End & Accept and Decline action sequences, call roles and hold values. The
  loader requires unique phases, successful wait outcomes and matching ordered
  execute/perform events. The failed `ui7` attempt is an explicit negative control.
  CLI receipts bind those expectations to one journal transaction and its ordered
  delegate actions by action ID. `call-waiting.jsonl` is not used for D5.
- Measured `inv` reference: both cleanup ends route to revived provider B.
  `providers.swift` iterates `observer.calls` during cleanup; the device's B-call
  then A-call order therefore does not establish a stable platform order.
  Conformance compares the complete semantic routing inventory and preserves
  each run's submitted cleanup request order. It rejects delivery reordering
  while accepting permutations of complete cleanup requests.
- Runtime policy: generation race checks have no device oracle and now label
  that limitation in their documentation and JSON result. Teardown callbacks
  remain uncharacterized pending R3 device measurement.
