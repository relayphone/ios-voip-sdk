# Code of conduct

We want contributors and users to participate without harassment. Be respectful, assume good intent while accepting correction, and discuss ideas and behavior rather than attacking people. Welcome different levels of experience and give constructive feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, publishing someone's private information without permission, and sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions or restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations. To report a concern privately, contact a maintainer through a private channel. If you do not have one, open an issue asking for a private contact without publishing the details. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical, and retaliation for a good-faith report is not acceptable.
